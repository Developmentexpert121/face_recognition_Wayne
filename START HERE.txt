QR PHOTO SORTER (OPTIMISED)
===========================

FIRST TIME ONLY
---------------
1. Double-click   setup.bat
   Takes a few minutes and needs internet. It installs everything and then
   checks both name-tag readers are working. Wait for "Setup complete".

   If it says Python 3.12 is missing, install it from python.org and TICK
   "Add python.exe to PATH" on the first screen, then run setup.bat again.

EVERY TIME AFTER THAT
---------------------
2. Double-click   run_sorter.bat


USING IT
--------
1 - Photos to sort   : your PROOFS folder (the small versions)
2 - Proof folder     : the folder containing 'sorted' and 'culled'
                       (usually named "Proof")
Full-size (optional) : your full-size/raw folder. Improves accuracy.

Then click "Preview (dry run)" FIRST. It shows exactly what would happen
and moves nothing. Only click "Sort photos" once the preview looks right.


WHAT TO EXPECT
--------------
A shoot of ~1,200 photos takes roughly 15-25 minutes depending on your PC.

You will see two stages:
   Step 1  reads QR codes on every photo        (about a minute)
   Step 2  reads the printed ID number, but only on photos still
           unidentified after step 1            (a few minutes)

Both stages are always on. Step 2 is what catches tags whose QR code is
bent, blurred or angled - it recovers students step 1 cannot.


IMPORTANT
---------
* Proofs must be FULL SIZE, around 2000x3000. A proof shrunk to 1000x666
  makes the tag about 15 pixels wide, which no software can read.

* The tag must be VISIBLE in at least one photo per student. On drape
  shoots the drape often covers it - pin it to the drape, or take one
  tag shot before the drape goes on.

* Tags shot straight-on read best. Heavily angled or curved tags are the
  ones most likely to need step 2.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
* "Step 2 UNAVAILABLE" in red  -> the text reader did not install.
  Run setup.bat again. Without it you will miss students.

* Very few students found      -> check the Proof folder is the one that
  directly contains 'sorted' and 'culled'.

* Nothing sorts at all         -> check your proofs are full size (above).
